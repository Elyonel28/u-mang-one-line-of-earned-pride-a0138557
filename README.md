## The line

> For nine years she carried the water up the hill each morning; today her daughter carried it, and she walked beside her empty-handed, watching her own strength walk ahead of her.

178 characters, one line. Nine years of effort, landing in a single scene: a mother, a hill, a daughter carrying the water now — the pride is earned inside the line, never announced.
